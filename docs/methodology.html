Methodology & Technical Details

Data sources, calculation methods, quality assessment, and analytical assumptions

Data Foundation

Primary Dataset

File: operational_intelligence_transactions_db.csv

  • Records: 62,035 aggregated transaction rows
  • Time Period: January 1 through March 22, 2025 (81 days, 90% of Q1)
  • Granularity: Daily aggregates by entity, product, price tier, anticipation method, payment method, and installments
  • Total TPV: R$ 19.2 billion
  • Transaction Count: 563,076 transactions analyzed

Key Fields Used

  • day, entity, product, price_tier
  • anticipation_method, nitro_or_d0, payment_method, installments
  • amount_transacted, quantity_transactions, quantitu_of_merchants

Data Quality Assessment

Issue: Primary dataset covers January 1 through March 22, 2025 (81 days), not complete Q1 through March 31.

Impact: "Q1 2025" references technically represent "January 1 through March 22". Monthly growth calculations exclude final 9 days of March. This does not materially affect strategic findings.

Mitigation: Patterns validated across 81 days representing 90% of Q1. Trends (PF growth, PIX stagnation, weekday patterns) validated across multiple months.

Issue: amount_transacted column has inconsistent decimal precision (1-2 decimal places).

Impact Calculation:

  • Maximum rounding error: R$ 1,123.10 (0.0000058% of total TPV)
  • Expected rounding error: R$ 561.55 (0.0000029% of total TPV)

Conclusion: Precision variation has negligible impact on aggregate totals and strategic conclusions. Less than 0.00001% of total TPV, immaterial for decision-making.

Issue: Primary datasets lack time-of-day granularity. Hourly data available only for 2-day operational snapshot.

Impact: 3AM anomaly (Finding #1) discovered in 2-day snapshot only. Cannot validate if pattern consistent across entire Q1.

Mitigation: 3AM anomaly present across both days of snapshot, suggesting systematic pattern. AI Ops Bot recommendation addresses need for continuous monitoring.

Issue: Column naming varies between datasets (e.g., quantitu_of_merchants vs quantity_of_merchants).

Impact: Code must handle multiple column name variations. Does not affect analysis validity but adds complexity.

Calculation Methodologies

Growth Calculations

Month-over-month growth calculated as:

(Current Month - Previous Month) / Previous Month × 100
Period TPV Days Change Growth %
January 2025 R$ 5.97B 31 — — (baseline)
February 2025 R$ 6.39B 28 +R$ 0.42B +7.1%
March 2025 (partial) R$ 6.85B 22 +R$ 0.46B +7.2%
Q1 Total (actual) R$ 19.20B 81 — 14.8% compound

Compound Growth Rate: 14.8% represents compound monthly growth rate from January through March (partial).

Based on Industry Benchmarks:

  • Kabbage: approximately 4.2x (acquired by American Express for $850M in 2020)
  • Square Capital: approximately 4.1x
  • Blend: approximately 3.8x

Calculation Method: Lending revenue per customer divided by transaction revenue per customer, multiplied by adoption rate.

Conservative Assumption: 25% merchant adoption, 3.5x average multiplier. Base case assumes 4.5x.

Percentage Point Changes

Direct subtraction method (e.g., 31.9% - 29.5% = 2.4 percentage points).

Budget Estimation Methodology

⚠️ Critical Disclaimer

All budget estimates are preliminary estimates based on external benchmarks and assumptions, not actual CloudWalk internal cost data. These estimates require validation with CloudWalk's finance, procurement, and operations teams before budget approval.

Industry Benchmarks Used:

  • Brazilian fintech industry cost benchmarks (marketing, development, operations)
  • Payment processing industry partnership revenue share standards (15-25%)
  • Financial services regulatory compliance costs (legal, capital adequacy assessments)
  • Technology development cost estimates (based on typical Brazilian development rates)

Data Limitations:

  • ❌ No access to CloudWalk internal cost structures, team capacity, existing budgets
  • ❌ No access to historical project costs for comparison
  • ❌ Partnership terms unknown (actual iFood/Uber negotiation outcomes)
  • ❌ Regulatory costs uncertain (depends on regulatory complexity)

Precision Limitations:

Budget ranges (e.g., R$ 2.5-3.8M) reflect estimation uncertainty. Actual costs may vary by ±30-50% depending on internal cost structures, negotiation outcomes, regulatory complexity, team availability, and market conditions.

Required Validation

Before implementation, CloudWalk should:

  1. Finance Team Review: Validate estimates against actual cost structures
  2. Procurement Review: Obtain actual vendor quotes
  3. Operations Review: Confirm team capacity and internal development costs
  4. Partnership Negotiation: Conduct preliminary discussions to validate assumptions
  5. Regulatory Consultation: Engage legal/compliance teams to refine cost estimates
