Unified Growth Strategy: Individual Merchant Capture Enabling PIX Adoption

The individual merchant (PF) segment grew 2.4 percentage points during Q1 without targeted marketing, while weekend volumes are 50% below weekday peak when these merchants are most active. Simultaneously, CloudWalk's PIX adoption remains at 13% compared to 43% nationally. This creates a sequential growth opportunity: capture individual merchants who naturally prefer PIX, then bundle PIX capabilities as the strategic next step.
