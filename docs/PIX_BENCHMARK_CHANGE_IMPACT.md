# Impact Assessment: Changing PIX Benchmark Comparison

## Current State (Incorrect Comparison)

**Current Comparison:**
- CloudWalk: 13% of total TPV is PIX
- National: 22% of PIX transactions are P2B
- Gap: 9 percentage points

**Problem:** Comparing apples to oranges (different denominators)

---

## Proposed Change (Option B - Correct Comparison)

**New Comparison:**
- CloudWalk: 13% of total TPV is PIX
- National: 43% of all payments use PIX
- Gap: **30 percentage points** (much larger!)

**Why This Makes Sense:**
- Same denominator structure (all payments / total payments)
- More accurate representation of underperformance
- Aligns with PIX being "Brazil's #1 payment method"

---

## Impact Analysis

### 1. Gap Magnitude Change

| Metric | Current (Wrong) | New (Correct) | Change |
|--------|----------------|---------------|---------|
| Benchmark | 22% (P2B PIX) | 43% (All PIX) | +21pp |
| Gap | 9 percentage points | **30 percentage points** | **+21pp** |
| Underperformance | Moderate | **Severe** | Much worse |

**Strategic Impact:**
- Changes narrative from "moderate gap" to "severe underperformance"
- Increases urgency of the finding
- Makes the opportunity even more compelling

### 2. Opportunity Size Recalculation

**Current Calculation (based on 22% target):**
- 13% → 22% = +9 percentage points
- Opportunity: ~R$ 1.7B TPV (mentioned in Finding #2)

**New Calculation (based on 43% benchmark):**
- 13% → 43% = +30 percentage points
- **But this is likely unrealistic as a target** - 43% is national average for ALL payments

**More Realistic Approach:**
- Use 43% as context (PIX dominance)
- Set realistic target (maybe 20-25% based on competitive benchmarks)
- Acknowledge 30pp gap as severity indicator, not immediate target

### 3. Strategic Urgency Level

**Current Narrative:**
- "9 percentage points below average" = Moderate concern

**New Narrative:**
- "30 percentage points below Brazil's #1 payment method" = Severe underperformance
- "13% vs 43% national average" = CloudWalk is dramatically underperforming

**Impact:**
- Increases urgency
- Strengthens business case
- Makes Finding #2 more compelling

### 4. Competitive Positioning

**Current:**
- Competitors "likely achieve 20-30% PIX share"

**New Context:**
- National average is 43%
- CloudWalk at 13% means:
  - If competitors are at 20-30%, they're still below national average but ahead of CloudWalk
  - CloudWalk is even further behind than initially stated

- "30pp gap means 30pp opportunity" (not directly achievable)

### Do Emphasize:
- "43% national adoption shows market acceptance"
- "30pp gap indicates severe underperformance"

- "Even achieving 20-25% represents significant growth"

## Recommended Approach

### Option 1: Direct Replacement (Simple)
- Replace 22% with 43% everywhere
- Replace "9pp gap" with "30pp gap"
- Keep target at 20% (realistic)
- Frame 43% as context/severity indicator

### Option 2: Nuanced Approach (More Accurate)
- Use 43% as national benchmark context
- Acknowledge product mix differences
- Set realistic target (20-25%)
- Frame: "30pp gap shows severity, but realistic target is 20-25%"

### Option 3: Dual Benchmark (Most Comprehensive)
- Show both comparisons:
  - "30pp below national average (43%) - shows market potential"
  - "Realistic target: 20-25% based on competitive benchmarks"
  - Explain why 43% isn't direct target (product mix, B2B transactions)

## Risk Assessment

### Risks of Changing:
1. **May seem unrealistic** - 30pp gap sounds too large
2. **Could demotivate** - "We'll never catch up" perception
3. **Credibility** - If we set 43% as target and miss, credibility suffers

### Mitigations:
1. **Use 43% as context**, not direct target
2. **Set realistic targets** (20-25%)
3. **Frame positively**: "National adoption shows market acceptance, CloudWalk has room to grow"
4. **Emphasize incremental progress**: "Even 13% → 20% is significant growth"

---

## Recommendation

**I recommend Option 2 (Nuanced Approach):**

1. **Update all references** from 22% P2B to 43% national average
2. **Change gap** from 9pp to 30pp where discussing severity
3. **Keep target** at 20-25% (realistic competitive benchmark)
4. **Add explanation**: "While 43% national average shows market adoption, realistic target accounts for CloudWalk's product mix"
5. **Frame positively**: Emphasize growth opportunity, not just gap

This maintains accuracy while keeping the finding actionable and credible.
